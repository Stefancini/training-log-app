Training Log: privacy and terms

Last updated 8 October 2026.

Privacy

Your log is stored only on this device, in this app's browser storage. Nothing is sent anywhere: no accounts, no analytics, no cookies. Exports are files you control. Clearing the app's site data deletes your log, so export first.

The app has no server. It is a web page you install; it does not collect, transmit or store any personal data outside your device. No accounts, analytics, advertising or cookies are used.
